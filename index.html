Chapter 01 / Origin / 2020–2023

I started by building worlds.

My first three years were at Cognizant, in XR. Every project had the same shape: a headset or a pair of smart glasses, a network that might drop, and a person who needed the thing to work on the first try.

  • VR trainingA de-escalation simulator for customer service teams. Sentiment analysis read the trainee and steered the angry caller's next move.
  • Remote AR assistA smart-glass app where a remote expert joins by video and draws AR annotations into the field worker's view.
  • Avatar toolkitA browser-based 3D avatar customiser built on react-three-fiber, pluggable into any engine that imports GLTF at runtime.
  • CAD pipelineAutomated CAD conversion that took a two-day manual job down to a few hours.
  • Learning gamesA set of legacy Flash maths games rebuilt in Unity for WebGL and iPad, delivered to production.

XR taught me to design the whole system at once: device, network, model and the human in the loop.
